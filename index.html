Our Philosophy

From Highland Farms to Your Morning Cup

Founded in 2018, Hearth & Roast partners directly with family-owned micro-lots in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. We roast on a vintage cast-iron drum roaster to highlight natural stone-fruit, cacao, and floral notes without bitterness.
